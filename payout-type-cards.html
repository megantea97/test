Request for Payment

Your customer approves a request in their own banking app.

  • The payment arrives in seconds, final.
  • Every request carries the amount, due date and expiry, plus a reference for automatic reconciliation.
  • Your customer stays in control: pay now, schedule or decline.
  • Available 24/7, where the customer’s bank supports Request for Payment.

ACH Debit

Your customer authorizes a debit from their bank or credit union account.

  • Online consumer debits are validated before the first debit.
  • Same Day ACH settles the same business day. Standard ACH settles the next.
  • Reaches all U.S. bank and credit union accounts.

Debit and Credit Cards

Your customer pays with a debit or credit card.

  • Approved in seconds.
  • Debit: paid from the customer’s linked bank account. Credit: paid on their credit line.
  • Payments settle to your account, typically the next business day.
  • Card payments can be disputed by the cardholder under card network rules.
