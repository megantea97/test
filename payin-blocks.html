Pay-in options

When is a payment final?

It depends how it was paid. Each method settles at a different time, and some can still be returned or disputed after they settle.

RTP and FedNow

Send a request and your customer approves it in their banking app. Funds arrive over RTP or FedNow in seconds, and they’re final.

Same day and standard

Debit your customer’s bank or credit union account with their authorization, same day or next. The ACH Network reaches every U.S. account. Funds can be returned within 60 days.

Card acceptance

Take card payments that are approved in seconds. Funds settle to your account the next business day. Funds can be disputed up to 540 days.
