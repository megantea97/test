Debit and credit cards

Approved in seconds. Settled after clearing.

Your customer pays with a card linked to their bank account, or on their credit line. Every card payment goes through three stages: authorization, clearing and settlement.

01

Debit

Paid from the customer’s linked bank account.

02

Credit

Paid on the customer’s credit line.

01

Authorization

Happens in seconds. The card issuer approves or declines the payment.

02

Clearing

Transaction details are exchanged.

03

Settlement

Funds reach your account, typically the next business day.

Card payments can be disputed by the cardholder under card network rules: generally up to 120 days from the transaction date, and up to 540 days in certain cases.
