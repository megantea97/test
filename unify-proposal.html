About Unify.Health

Unify.Health is an Athlete Health Exchange (AHE): a real-time interoperability network connecting athletic departments, clinical teams and partner providers to verified medical records across 4,400+ hospitals, 50,000+ clinics, 600,000+ providers and 261M+ unique patient records in the U.S. Through direct integration with Epic, Cerner, Oracle Health, CVS Health and other primary EHR networks, Unify retrieves longitudinal records on query; aggregated, deduplicated, normalized and consent-governed.

We integrate with existing AMS and EHR platforms via standard APIs. HIPAA-compliant, audit-logged end to end, and a vetted implementer with the primary EHR systems in the U.S.
