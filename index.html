Same Day ACH

Same business day, within hours
  • Three same-day processing windows.
  • Up to $1 million per payment, rising to $10 million on September 17, 2027.
  • 1.4 billion Same Day ACH payments worth $3.9 trillion in 2025.

Standard ACH

Next business day
  • Suited to recurring and lower-urgency payments where next-day settlement is enough.

How ACH Debit works

1

Your customer authorizes the debit, typically online, and provides or links their account.

2

For online consumer debits, the account is validated before the first debit.

3

The debit is submitted for a Same Day ACH window or for standard settlement.

4

Funds settle on the same banking day or on the next banking day.
